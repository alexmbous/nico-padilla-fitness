About Nico

Photo

With years of experience in personal training, I'm passionate about helping people of all ages build strength, improve mobility, and live healthier lives.

Whether you're an athlete looking to push your limits or a senior seeking to maintain independence and vitality, I create personalized programs tailored to your unique goals.

I specialize in strength training and geriatric fitness, working with clients both at the gym and in the comfort of their own homes across the San Antonio area. My approach is built on patience, expertise, and a genuine commitment to your progress.

  • Personalized training programs
  • In-home & gym-based sessions
  • Strength training for all levels
  • Senior-focused fitness & mobility
